# Win-Your-Money-Back Designer

Designs a pay-up-front, hit-the-goal, fee-returns attraction offer whose criteria drive the behaviour that produces the result.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Only for effort-dependent results (coaching, habit, education) or dormant-list reactivation. Criteria must be outcome-linked and ungameable; check-ins built in as the upsell moments.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Win-Your-Money-Back Designer to help me: designs a pay-up-front, hit-the-goal, fee-returns attraction offer whose criteria drive the behaviour that produces the result — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
